PTD Signal Dispatcher

This page is the public home of the PTD Signal Dispatcher, a private automation application operated by PaperTrailDebunks studio.

The dispatcher routes production signals between the studio's own departments. It is operated by the studio owner, on the studio's own accounts, and is not offered as a service to the public. There is no sign-up, no third-party use, and no public access to the application.
